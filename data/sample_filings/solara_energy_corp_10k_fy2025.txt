ITEM 7. MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS

Consolidated revenue for fiscal year 2025 was $1.24 billion, an increase
of 28% compared to $969 million in fiscal year 2024. Growth was led by
our Utility-Scale Solar segment, which grew 34% year-over-year to $780
million as demand for grid-scale renewable capacity accelerated. Our
Residential Solar segment grew 19% year-over-year to $340 million. Our
Storage Systems segment grew 22% year-over-year to $120 million.

ITEM 7A. QUANTITATIVE AND QUALITATIVE DISCLOSURES ABOUT MARKET RISK

Our credit agreement requires us to maintain a maximum leverage ratio
(total debt to EBITDA) of 3.0x. As of December 31, 2025, our leverage
ratio was 1.8x, well within the covenant limit. Our credit agreement
also requires a minimum interest coverage ratio of 3.0x; we reported
5.2x for the period, a substantial improvement from the prior year's
4.1x, reflecting strong earnings growth outpacing debt service costs.
