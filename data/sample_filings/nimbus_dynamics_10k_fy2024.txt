ITEM 7. MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS

Consolidated revenue for fiscal year 2024 was $898 million, an increase
of 11% compared to $809 million in fiscal year 2023. Growth was led by
our Cloud Infrastructure segment, which grew 18% year-over-year to $361
million on strong enterprise renewal rates. Managed Services grew 6%
year-over-year to $376 million. Data Analytics, our newest segment,
grew 40% year-over-year to $100 million from a small base.

ITEM 7A. QUANTITATIVE AND QUALITATIVE DISCLOSURES ABOUT MARKET RISK

Our credit agreement requires us to maintain a maximum leverage ratio
(total debt to EBITDA) of 3.5x. As of December 31, 2024, our leverage
ratio was 2.9x, comfortably within the covenant limit. Our credit
agreement also requires a minimum interest coverage ratio of 2.5x; we
reported 3.4x for the period, reflecting healthy debt service capacity.
