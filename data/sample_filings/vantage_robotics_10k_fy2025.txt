ITEM 7. MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS

Consolidated revenue for fiscal year 2025 was $412 million, a decline of
11% compared to $463 million in fiscal year 2024. The decline was
primarily driven by our Industrial Automation segment, which fell 19%
year-over-year to $260 million amid reduced capital spending by
manufacturing customers. Our Service & Maintenance segment grew 4%
year-over-year to $152 million, partially offsetting the decline.

ITEM 7A. QUANTITATIVE AND QUALITATIVE DISCLOSURES ABOUT MARKET RISK

Our credit agreement requires us to maintain a maximum leverage ratio
(total debt to EBITDA) of 4.0x. As of December 31, 2025, our leverage
ratio was 4.3x, in breach of the covenant limit; the Company has
obtained a temporary waiver from its lenders while it pursues an
amendment to the credit agreement. Our credit agreement also requires a
minimum interest coverage ratio of 2.0x; we reported 1.6x for the
period, also below the required minimum and subject to the same waiver.

ITEM 3. LEGAL PROCEEDINGS

The Company is a defendant in a breach-of-contract action brought by a
former customer alleging failure to deliver contracted automation
equipment on schedule. The plaintiff seeks damages of approximately $18
million. The Company disputes the claims and intends to defend
vigorously; based on information currently available, no reasonable
estimate of loss can be made at this time.
